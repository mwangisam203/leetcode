OPTIONAL STUDY SCHEDULE

Adjust the pace to your review needs; the week numbers are a suggested order,
not completed work or deadlines. See pattern-roadmap.txt for recognition cues.

Topics about DSA

Order of topics
Go roughly in this sequence — each builds on the last:

Arrays & Strings — two pointers, sliding window, prefix sums
Hashing — hash maps/sets for O(1) lookups (this unlocks a huge chunk of "easy" problems)
Linked Lists — traversal, reversal, fast/slow pointers
Stacks & Queues — monotonic stacks, parentheses matching, BFS prep
Recursion & Backtracking — before trees, since trees are recursive by nature
Trees — traversals (in/pre/post-order), BST properties, then binary tree problems
Graphs — BFS, DFS, then topological sort, union-find
Heaps / Priority Queues — top-K problems, merging
Dynamic Programming — save for last; it's the hardest and benefits from everything above (especially recursion and arrays)
Greedy & Intervals — can be woven in anytime after arrays


Topics coverage by week

Week-by-week
Week 1 — Arrays, Strings, Two Pointers, Sliding Window

Days 1–2: Two pointers (reverse array, valid palindrome, container with most water)
Days 3–4: Sliding window (maximum sum of a fixed-size subarray, longest substring without repeats)
Days 5–6: Prefix sums (subarray sum equals K, running sum)
~12–15 problems total


Week 2 — Hashing + Binary Search

Days 1–2: Hash maps/sets (two sum, group anagrams, longest consecutive sequence)
Days 3–4: Binary search on sorted arrays (classic + search in rotated array)
Days 5–6: Binary search on answer space (this is the harder, less intuitive version — e.g. "minimum capacity to ship packages in D days")
~12 problems

Week 3 — Linked Lists + Stacks/Queues

Days 1–2: Linked list traversal, reversal, cycle detection (Floyd's)
Days 3–4: Merge two sorted lists, remove Nth node, fast/slow pointer patterns
Days 5–6: Stacks (valid parentheses, min stack), monotonic stack (daily temperatures)
~12 problems

Week 4 — Recursion + Backtracking

Days 1–2: Pure recursion warm-ups (subsets, permutations)
Days 3–5: Backtracking (combination sum, N-Queens, word search)
Day 6: Review — redo 2 problems from Week 1 cold, no notes
~10 problems + 1 review day


Week 5 — Trees

Days 1–2: Traversals (recursive + iterative), depth/height
Days 3–4: BST-specific (validate BST, insert/search, LCA)
Days 5–6: Tree + backtracking combo (path sum, all root-to-leaf paths)
~12 problems

Week 6 — Graphs

Days 1–2: BFS/DFS on grids and adjacency lists (number of islands, clone graph)
Days 3–4: Topological sort (course schedule)
Days 5–6: Union-Find (redundant connection, number of provinces)
~10 problems


Week 7 — Heaps + Greedy/Intervals

Days 1–2: Heap basics (kth largest, top K frequent)
Days 3–4: Merge intervals, meeting rooms
Days 5–6: Greedy (jump game, gas station)
~10 problems

Weeks 8–10 — Dynamic Programming (give this the most time)

Week 8: 1D DP (climbing stairs, house robber, coin change)
Week 9: 2D DP (unique paths, longest common subsequence, edit distance)
Week 10: DP on trees/knapsack variants + full review — redo one hard problem from each earlier topic cold
~25–30 problems across the three weeks
